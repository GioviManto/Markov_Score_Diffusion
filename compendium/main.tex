\noindent\textbf{About this volume.} This is the complete, extended version
of the thesis of the same title; the submitted thesis is its concise form.
It carries every derivation written out, the step-by-step toolbox
derivations, the rotating-ring precursor study, and the full reproducibility
and aggregation appendices, and it is the reference for any result the
concise thesis states without proof. Its content is that of the extended
build preserved at the repository tag \texttt{extended-thesis-pre-compression};
chapter, section and equation numbers therefore differ from those of the
submitted thesis, and every quantitative result is produced by the same
frozen-output pipeline. Where this volume mentions ``the archived technical
compendium'' it refers to the earlier, superseded technical compendium
(September 2026), kept in the \texttt{archive/} folder of the distribution.
\end{minipage}
