\section{Build identifier and figure-to-code map}

outputs under \texttt{research/outputs/frozen/}; none is typed by hand
(Table~\ref{tab:figure-map}). The quoted experiment numbers are defined once
as macros in \texttt{sections/} and cited by name, so tables, figures and
text share one aggregation rule by construction. The build manifest records
the state this document was made from: repository revision \bmreposha{},
generated inputs \bmgenerated{} over \bmgeneratedcount{} files, figures

source side only; the provenance of the frozen outputs is disclosed below.

of this thesis: the same results with every derivation written out.

\section{Provenance and protocol facts}\label{sec:repro-provenance}\label{sec:repro-learning}

The closed-form and quadrature studies are deterministic and run on a laptop;
the estimator comparisons ran under SLURM from the scripts in \texttt{hpc/},
and four provenance routes appear, in decreasing order of strength.
\emph{Certified:} \texttt{exp\_31} (screen and confirmatory comparison) and
\texttt{exp\_32} (capacity) were deployed by a script that refuses a dirty
tree, ships a \texttt{git archive} of the commit and records its digest.
\emph{Legacy digest, empty intersection:} \texttt{exp\_07}, the headline
comparison, predates that script and is citable because the intersection of
each run's recorded dirty-file list with its own import closure is empty, a
check recomputed on every regeneration; the one row where it was not,
$\nseq = 8192$, is withdrawn. \emph{Recorded configuration only:} the
validation and diagnostic runs (\texttt{exp\_01}, \texttt{02}, \texttt{06},
\texttt{18}, \texttt{22}, \texttt{27}) carry the resolved configuration and
seeds but an empty revision field, and the claims resting on them are stated
as measured curves and orderings. \emph{Certified after rerun:}
\texttt{exp\_21}, behind Table~\ref{tab:nonmarkov}, is a clean-deployment
rerun whose Gaussian arm reproduces the run it replaces on every quoted
number.

Both arms of the headline comparison select their budget on a disjoint
validation bundle, agreeing with the test-set optimum in $\netagree\%$ and
$\emagree\%$ of cells; rerunning $\nseq = \nseqcalib$ at the raised budget of
the $\nseq = \extendedn$ row moves its pooled ratio by
$\budgetdelta \pm \budgetdeltase$, in the direction that makes the capped
table conservative for the structured arm. The resolution gate excludes
$\ndropped$ of $\ncellstotal$ cells whose narrowest fitted component is under
two grid cells wide; including them changes no ratio by more than
$\dropshift$. The screen ran $\screenconfigs$ configurations over
$\screendevseeds$ development seeds disjoint from the confirmatory seeds, with
learning rates \screenlrs{}, both output heads and a checkpoint ladder from
$\screenckptlo$ to $\screenckpthi$ steps. The settling times of
Section~\ref{sec:em-stopping-times} come from the $\convcap$-iteration shard
of \texttt{exp\_27}, the only run length at which no configuration settles at
its own cap. The environment is Python $\ge 3.10$ with NumPy, SciPy, pandas
and Matplotlib, float64 throughout, the neural baselines in pure NumPy with
an explicit random generator so that training is reproducible from the
recorded seed; these are version floors rather than a lock, so byte-level
reproduction is not claimed, only determinism given the seeds on a fixed
installation. The document compiles with one run of
\texttt{tectonic main.tex}, and \texttt{check.sh} reports unresolved
references, undefined citations, duplicate labels and overfull boxes.

\section{How the reported ratios were aggregated}\label{sec:aggregation-headline}

A ratio is a nonlinear function of two means, so it depends on the order in
which averaging is done, and both reported ratios were recomputed under
alternative aggregations of the same frozen cells, refitting nothing. For
the headline (Table~\ref{tab:pointwise}) the reported estimand is the
schedule-pooled paired per-seed ratio of \eqref{eq:em-pooling}; the mean of
per-(seed, level) ratios, which earlier drafts reported, is systematically
the largest alternative ($\ratiocellwiselo$--$\ratiocellwisehi\times$ against
the pooled $\ratiolo$--$\ratiohi\times$), because a per-level ratio at a level
with little reference energy can be very large while contributing little.
The direction is the same under every aggregation, every paired bootstrap
interval over seeds sits well clear of one, and no aggregation produces a
reversal at any size; resolved by noise level, the per-level ratio peaks near
$t \approx 0.5$, and EM--BP is beaten outright in $\nreversed$ of
$\ncellsused$ cells. For the structure-aware comparison the reported estimand
is not uniformly the largest, but the floor across the four aggregations is
$\structaggfloor\times$ with a spread of $\structaggspread$
(Table~\ref{tab:aggregation-structured}), so the reading of a low single-digit
multiple at the smallest training set growing to roughly six at the largest
survives every one of them.
