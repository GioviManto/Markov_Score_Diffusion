During the preparation of this thesis the author used large language models
--- Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT, and Google's Gemini --- as
assistive tools, in the following capacities:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Software development.} Assistance in writing, refactoring and
  documenting the analysis, experiment-management and figure-generation code
  of the accompanying research repository, including the \LaTeX{} build and
  consistency-checking tooling.
  \item \textbf{Manuscript review.} Reading drafts of the manuscript to flag
  potential mathematical slips, inconsistencies between text, tables and
  figures, and unclear passages, which the author then checked and resolved.
  \item \textbf{Language and typesetting.} Editing prose for clarity and
  concision, and assistance with \LaTeX{} formatting.
\end{itemize}

No experimental result, number, table entry or figure in this document was
produced by a language model: all reported quantities are computed by the
version-controlled pipeline described in Appendix~\ref{app:repro} from the
frozen experiment outputs it documents, and the figures and generated tables
are built by the scripts that appendix lists. The research questions, the
mathematical derivations, the experimental design and the conclusions are the
author's, every AI-assisted change was reviewed by the author before
inclusion, and the author takes full responsibility for the entire content of
this thesis.
