In preparing this thesis the author used three large language models ---
Anthropic's Claude \citep{anthropic2025claude}, OpenAI's ChatGPT
\citep{openai2025chatgpt} and Google's Gemini \citep{google2025gemini} --- as
assistive tools, in three capacities: writing, refactoring and documenting the
analysis, experiment-management and figure-generation code of the research
repository, including the \LaTeX{} build and consistency checks; reading
drafts to flag possible mathematical slips, inconsistencies between text,
tables and figures, and unclear passages; and editing prose for clarity and
assisting with \LaTeX{} typesetting.

The research questions, the mathematical derivations, the experimental
design, the interpretation of the results and the conclusions are the
author's, every AI-assisted change was reviewed by the author before
inclusion, and the author takes full responsibility for the content. No
number, table entry or figure in this document was produced by a language
model: all reported quantities are computed by the version-controlled
pipeline of Appendix~\ref{app:repro} from the frozen experiment outputs it
documents, and the figures and generated tables are built by the scripts that
appendix lists.
