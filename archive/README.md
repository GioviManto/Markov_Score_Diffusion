- `technical-compendium-2026-09-12.pdf` and `technical-compendium-2026-09-12-sources.zip`:
  the earlier technical compendium (*Diffusion Scores for Markov Data: a
  technical compendium*, 114 pp), which carried the background derivations
  cut from the thesis, the experiment ledger and the claim audit (its
  Chapter 11, twenty-one entries on what was settled and what was withdrawn,
  including the retracted reverse-generation comparison). It is superseded as
  the companion volume by `../compendium/`, the extended research record of
  the thesis; references to "the archived technical compendium" in that
  volume point here.
- The extended thesis in its original submission format (230 pp, with the
  university's four mandatory front pages) is preserved at the git tag
  `extended-thesis-pre-compression`; `../compendium/` is the same document
  with a title page in place of those pages.
